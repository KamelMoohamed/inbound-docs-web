# Case study template (real customers)

Use this to produce a **truthful, publishable** case study from a real customer. Every
number must be verifiable, and any quote must be a real, attributed quote the customer has
signed off on. See the legal note at the bottom before you publish.

---

## Title
> `How <Practice> cut <metric> by <X>%` — lead with the single most compelling, verified result.

## The challenge
3–5 specific, concrete pain points the practice had **before** CliniDoc. Use their words
where possible.

## The solution
How they connected CliniDoc, how it works for them, and the one thing that surprised them.

## Results at a glance (4 stat tiles)
Each stat must be measured, with the source noted internally:
- Auto-ready rate (%)
- Staff time saved (per day/week)
- Documents lost (count)
- Average review time

## Before / after (table)
Paired rows contrasting the old workflow with the new one.

## Implementation timeline
Day-by-day, with realistic durations.

## Privacy & compliance
Onshore storage, audit log, encryption, AI-use disclosure.

## Quote(s)
Real, attributed, signed-off. e.g. *"…" — Practice Manager, <Practice>*

## CTA
Start free / Book a demo.

### ⚠️ Legal & ethics checklist before publishing
- [ ] **Written consent** from the practice to be named (or to be described anonymously).
- [ ] **Every metric is verifiable** — keep the source/measurement on file.
- [ ] **Quotes are real and approved** — no fabricated or composite testimonials.
- [ ] **No misleading impression** — the result must be representative, not cherry-picked
      in a way that implies typical outcomes it can't support (Australian Consumer Law).
- [ ] **No patient-identifying information** in any example.
- [ ] If anonymised, the page still makes clear it is a genuine customer, not invented.

> Until a real case study clears this checklist, publish the illustrative version
> (`/customers`) clearly labelled as an example.
